# Council mode

For a question or decision that benefits from opposing views ("council", or the user's word for it in the profile). lifecycle.md applies; there is no pilot (one prompt per worker).

1. Pick three workers from three different families (qc.md 4 lists them), subscriptions first; never Claude subagents, which are Claude's own family. Assign the stances: for, against, neutral. For a small question two workers (for and against) are enough; say so in the plan.
2. Send each the same context and question with its stance prompt (templates.md 6), all in parallel. No worker sees another's answer.
3. Claude synthesizes, in the user's language: where they agree; the real disagreements; and which side the evidence supports, with the source or reasoning behind it. No invented consensus and no majority vote: a point stands on its evidence, not on how many workers made it. Arguments that cannot be traced to evidence are labeled as opinion.
4. Report: which worker argued which stance, the synthesis, and what stays open.
